Privacy Policy

Sadqah Media · Last updated 30 October 2026

Sadqah Media runs the YouTube channels One More Sadqah, Islam for Little Ones and Solve by Sunrise, and related Instagram accounts. This policy explains what our publishing tool ("Sadqah Media Uploader") does with data.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the channel owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

What data the tool accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, the tool accesses only the owner's own channels in order to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions;
  • read and reply to comments on the owner's own videos;
  • read basic channel and video statistics.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not collect personal data from viewers. Channels marked "made for kids" have comments disabled by YouTube, and we never collect data from children.
  • We do not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation settings and are used only to perform the actions listed above. Public comments that the tool reads are processed to draft replies and are not stored beyond what is needed to reply.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own Google Privacy Policy also applies when you use YouTube.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
